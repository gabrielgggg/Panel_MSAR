\section{Model}
Seasonally adjusted real GDP per worker, 2026-09-17 vintage, 1985Q1 through 2019Q4. United States, Germany, and Japan are excluded. China is not in this vintage. No country random effects and no catch-up term.
\begin{align}
y_{it} &= a + g\, t + z_{it}, \\
z_{i,t+1} &= \bigl(1-\rho(s_{it})\bigr)\mu(s_{it}) + \rho(s_{it})\, z_{it} + \sigma(s_{it})\,\varepsilon_{it}, \\
s_{i,t+1} &\sim \Pi(\,\cdot\mid s_{it}).
\end{align}
Three regimes. $E[z]=0$ (the median regime mean is not pinned at 0). $\sigma$ and $\rho$ switch, with $|\rho|<0.99$. Standard errors are delta-method from a numerical Hessian.

\section{Common intercept and linear trend, 1985--2019, ex US, Germany, Japan, China}
